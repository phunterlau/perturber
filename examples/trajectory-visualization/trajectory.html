INTERPRETATION BOUNDARY

Read the sequence, not just the peak

The paired readout is an observational localization aid. The patch overlays become local causal evidence only through each immutable intervention run's recorded qualification, matched controls, and claim status. A downstream rise does not mean that the intervened signal is conserved; later blocks can transform or amplify it.
